# 🏋️ GymApp - Exercise GIF Animation Prompts for Gemini

This document contains pre-configured, consistent prompt recipes for generating seamless 5-second monochrome looping GIF animations for all default exercises in **GymApp**.

---

## 📌 Master Prompt Template (Variables in **`[BOLD BRACKETS]`**)

Use this template to generate consistent animations for any new or custom exercise:

```text
Create a seamless, perfectly closed 5-second looping GIF animation demonstrating the exercise: **[EXERCISE NAME]**.

Style & Aesthetics:
- Minimalist high-contrast monochrome / black and white line art with smooth anatomical shading.
- Neutral pure dark/charcoal background (**[BACKGROUND COLOR, default: #121212]**) with crisp white/light gray outline and silhouette for the lifter and gym equipment.
- Clean vector/technical fitness illustration style with no visual clutter, no watermark, no text overlays, and no facial distractions (faceless or minimalist stylized athlete).

Anatomy & Biomechanics:
- Accurate biomechanics, proper gym form, full range of motion.
- Target muscle groups: **[TARGET MUSCLE GROUPS]** with a subtle glowing/highlighted stroke during peak contraction.
- Form cue to emphasize: **[KEY FORM CUE]**.

Motion & Timing:
- Total duration: Exactly **[TOTAL DURATION, default: 5.0 seconds]** for 1 complete repetition cycle.
- Cadence: **[CONCENTRIC TIME, default: 2.0 seconds]** concentric (lifting) phase, **[HOLD TIME, default: 0.5 seconds]** peak contraction hold, **[ECCENTRIC TIME, default: 2.5 seconds]** eccentric (lowering) phase.
- Seamless loop: The exact starting posture matches the ending posture so the GIF loops infinitely without stutter or jump cuts.

Composition & Specifications:
- Aspect Ratio: **[ASPECT RATIO, default: 1:1]**
- Resolution: **[RESOLUTION, default: 512x512 px]**
- Camera Angle: **[CAMERA ANGLE]**, static camera with centered framing and margin space around the lifter and equipment.
```
